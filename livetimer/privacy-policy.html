中文

LiveTimer（以下简称「本 App」）非常重视你的隐私。本政策说明本 App 如何处理信息。

1. 我们不收集个人信息

本 App 不收集、不存储、不上传任何可识别你个人身份的信息。你无需注册或登录即可使用全部功能。

2. 本地数据

你在 App 内创建的抢购计时、设置等数据仅保存在你的设备本地（通过系统 UserDefaults），不会上传到任何服务器，也不会与第三方共享。卸载 App 即清除。

3. 网络请求

为校准设备时间，本 App 会向公开的 NTP 时间服务器及 HTTPS 站点发起请求。请求不会包含你的账号资料、倒计时内容或设置。与一般网络访问相同，相关服务提供方可能接收到 IP 地址等必要的连接信息，并依其各自政策处理；LiveTimer 开发者不会通过这些请求存储或追踪你。

4. 第三方服务

本 App 不集成第三方广告、分析或追踪 SDK，不进行跨 App / 跨网站的用户追踪。时间校准使用公共时间服务。

5. 通知与实时活动

本 App 的抢购提醒使用 iOS 本地通知，计时实时活动由 iOS 在设备上处理；本 App 不使用服务器向你发送远程推送。

6. 儿童隐私

本 App 不以儿童为对象收集个人信息，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

7. 政策更新

如本政策有更新，将在本页面发布最新版本。

8. 联系我们

如有任何隐私相关问题，请联系：gurui@moonrabbit.com.cn

English

LiveTimer ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. We do not collect personal information.

The App does not collect, store, or upload any personally identifiable information. No account or login is required to use any feature.

2. Local data.

Any timers and settings you create are stored locally on your device (via the system UserDefaults). They are never uploaded to any server nor shared with third parties, and are removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Network requests.

To calibrate the device clock, the App contacts public NTP time servers and HTTPS websites. Requests do not include your account details, timer content, or settings. As with ordinary network access, service providers may receive necessary connection information such as your IP address and process it under their own policies. The LiveTimer developer does not store or track you through these requests.

4. Third-party services.

The App integrates no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs and performs no cross-app or cross-site user tracking. Time calibration uses public time services.

5. Notifications and Live Activities.

Flash-sale reminders use local iOS notifications, and timer Live Activities are handled on your device by iOS. The App does not use a server to send remote push notifications.

6. Children's privacy.

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children and does not knowingly do so.

7. Changes.

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page.

8. Contact.

For any privacy questions, contact: gurui@moonrabbit.com.cn
